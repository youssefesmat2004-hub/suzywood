## AI search: ready to read, not proven to recommend

The current review confirms fully rendered pages, open crawler rules, and clean markdown access for AI assistants. Arabic FAQ content is present in the initial page response, not only after switching languages.

That does **not** prove ChatGPT, Gemini, or Perplexity recommends Suzy Wood. No AI citation ranking or AI-referral measurement was established in this review. The existing AI guidance file incorrectly labels `/book` as a measurement visit and invites custom builds; it is unchanged pending your separate approval.

The most useful next content improvements are verified tent dimensions, exactly what comes in the bundle, optional extras, genuine customer photos, and clear measurement information in both languages. Some current tent text conflicts on dimensions and included pompoms, so those facts need your confirmation before rewriting them. Do not invent certifications or safety claims.

Arabic currently shares the English URL and defaults to English during initial rendering; a complete Arabic catalog with separately addressable Arabic pages would be a larger follow-up, not something this review silently installed.
